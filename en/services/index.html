Clear arrangements, made in advance

All rates are per hour, with a minimum of 3 hours per appointment. Planned babysitting at home is €17/hour; last-minute care is €18/hour. Care at special occasions, family emergency support and care around a new arrival are €25/hour.

There is a €10 travel charge per visit for the round trip within my usual service area. If the appointment ends at 23:00 or later, you also pay for my taxi home. A paid taxi home is a condition for these appointments, agreed in advance. For other locations, I confirm travel costs beforehand.

You can cancel free of charge until one hour before the agreed start time. If you cancel within that final hour, you pay for one hour at the agreed hourly rate instead of the full appointment. If I cancel within one hour of the start time, the first hour of your next babysitting appointment lasting more than four hours is on me.

I carry out the babysitting myself. There is no extra charge per child: we discuss whether I can safely care for the number of children, their ages and needs on my own. I am based in Amsterdam and may also travel to Haarlem, Hoofddorp, Zaandam and Almere depending on the date, time and journey.

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. I first check availability and what is possible. Where appropriate, we have a short introduction, then I confirm the hours, rate, travel arrangements and cancellation policy in writing.

The website is also available in Arabic. I understand Arabic, but do not speak it myself. When we first meet, we make sure we understand each other, so we can make clear arrangements for your child.
